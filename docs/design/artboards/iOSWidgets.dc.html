01 · Notes · what's wrong with the current widgets and the rules for v2

Why the widgets look out of date

  • Grey boxes with grey kickers. Every widget is the old card grey with a caps kicker on top, the look D2 removed from the app.
  • Nothing leads. The sport is a 28 pt square icon. The app now opens every page with a visual; the widget opens with a date label.
  • The medium widget is three equal thirds. Session, week hours and food compete, so none of them reads at a glance.
  • Colour without meaning. Kcal to go is amber (a warning colour) even when you're on track. Week hours are white, but time is yellow everywhere in D2.
  • No shape to the week. You see hours, not which days are done. The Plan tab and the web both think in days.
  • Thin states. A rest day says "Recover well" and doesn't mention tomorrow. Done is a small tick. Sick days have no state at all.

v2 rules

  • The sport leads. Sport and focus as a coloured label over the title, as on activity detail. Rides and runs can add the workout profile. Round 2: ring and body figure dropped after review.
  • One title, one line. Italic Barlow title, one plain line under it. Only the top-left date stays as a kicker.
  • Colour per metric, same as D2. Time yellow ●, protein teal ●, sports in their sport colour, kcal bar volt as on the Food tab.
  • Volt only for wins. Done, target hit. Amber only when you really are behind (evening, under 60% eaten).
  • The week as seven dots. Filled = done in its sport colour, ring = planned, dim ring = missed, dash = rest. Today is underlined.
  • Flat, page-black tile. #0A0C11, no border, no gradient. The Lock Screen stays monochrome (iOS draws it that way), so shape carries meaning there, not colour.
