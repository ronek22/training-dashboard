Notes for the build
What changes
  • Header: week strip with target dots replaces the ‹ day buttons. Swipe the strip for earlier weeks.
  • Hero: eaten / target and a ring with kcal to go in the middle (solid = eaten, thin arc = planned dinner), and three macro columns replace the bar + tiles card.
  • Meals: one timeline card per entry with P/C/F chips; latest expanded with its items. Planned dinner gets Log as planned / Ate something else.
  • Scan: custom camera screen (AVFoundation) instead of the system picker, so modes, barcode detection and brackets are possible.
Backend (already there)
  • Meal photo / text → POST /phone/meal-estimate (as now).
  • Barcode → GET /nutrition/products?q=<code> first, then /nutrition/products/search?q=<code> (OFF); save with POST /nutrition/products.
  • Recent / Saved → /nutrition/food/saved + /products/{id}/use.
  • Label photo → needs a phone route that reads the nutrition table (web does this today). New endpoint.
  • Week dots → one /nutrition/food?date= per day, or a light week summary route.
Open questions
  • Keep thumbnails of meal photos? Needs the photo stored server-side (today it isn't). Without it, the row shows the meal emoji.
  • Scanner dots: on-device Vision finds the plate outline and food regions only. The real breakdown still comes from Claude after the shot.
  • Ship in two steps: 1. day screen + result (01, 04); 2. scanner modes + quick add (02, 03, 05).
