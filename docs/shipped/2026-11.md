### Winter arc editor and coach suggestion

2026-11-05: The Winter arc editor on Plan is a picture of the winter instead of a 10-column table, and the coach can draft the arc.
- Empty arc: "Suggest with coach" (primary) or "Set up myself" (template). In the editor, an optional note ("a trip, busy weeks, a goal…") goes with "Suggest with coach" / "Ask again".
- Chart: one bar per week, height = ride hours, coloured by block focus; lighter weeks are paler. Your last 8 weeks sit in grey to the left of the start, with a dashed "you ride ~2.5 h" line (6-week average). Block lanes under the bars carry name, hours and ride mix; months below.
- Clicking a bar or lane opens that block: inline name, focus segments, weeks and hours steppers, ride steppers per type (Z2, Sweet spot, VO2), lighter week Off / every 3 / 4 / 5, move earlier / later and remove. The coach's one-line reason shows on each block it suggested. The footer has the start Monday, Add block, Template, the total ("16 weeks · 57 h on the bike"), Cancel and Save arc.
- Suggestion: `GET /plans/winter-arc/suggestion-context` (`services/arc_suggest.py`) gives the last 12 full weeks (ride hours, rides, runs, lifts), FTP, active goals with commitment, illness in the last 60 days, life-load weeks ahead and the window (this or next Monday to the last Sunday of February). The coach helper's `/arc-suggestion` job (`scripts/arc_helper.py`, configured coach CLI) asks for back-to-back blocks that start near recent hours, keep anchor goals and fit the window, then clamps the reply to the editor's limits. Nothing is saved until Save arc.
- Verified with `test_arc_suggest.py` (3), `scripts/test_arc_helper.py` (6) and `tests/arc-draft.test.mjs` (5); a live suggestion at 1512 px returned Base → Sweet-spot build ×2 → VO2 touch → Maintain from 3 h, each with a reason. The arc was not saved.
