Notes
What changes and why
Layout
  • Before: three full-height year cards (~3 screens), then Recovery.
  • After: weekly hours, a 3-row year card and Recovery, all within ~2.5 screens. The first screen answers "how is my training going lately?".
  • One big title only. The section titles are smaller (26 pt).
  • Optional: a one-line summary under the title, built from the same numbers.
Charts
  • HRV and resting HR show a 7-day average line over faint daily dots, so night-to-night noise stops hiding the trend. The big number is the average, with "last night" under it.
  • Resting HR uses neutral slate. Only the pill is green or red.
  • Sleep gets a stage legend (Deep / REM / Core) with last night's split.
  • HRV and RHR share a row at half width. Steps becomes a slim strip with a 10k line.
Data
  • New GET /metrics/trends: 12 weeks of hours by sport group, plus year-to-date and same-date-last-year totals per sport. Existing routes stay as they are.
  • The month detail adds last year's months to that response (or training-history?year=2025).
  • Hide the vs-2025 pill when 2025 has under ~5% of this year's volume (history only starts Dec 2024).
  • Numbers here are live from the backend on 2 Nov.
