Full entries for features that shipped this month. `docs/current-state.md` keeps a one-line index; the Roadmap page's Shipped list is parsed from these files (`services/planning_status.py`).

### Week brief and weekly lift slots

2026-11-05: One ordered contract per week replaces a dozen separate planning inputs that could contradict each other (a quality-ride goal beat the Base block; an injury rule said no tempo while a knee-gated tempo was planned).
- `services/week_brief.py`, `GET /plans/week-brief?week_start=`: rules in a fixed order, Safety > Anchor > Block > Outcome > Habit, each with its source. Safety: active recovery issues (a knee, hip or heel takes the lower-body lift out), sick, illness return and mountain re-entry days. Anchor: anchor goals, with the lift slots spelled out. Block: the arc's ride hours and mix ("no sweet spot or VO2" in a base block). Outcome: other goals, resolved against the block ("waits for a quality block", "the block's 3.5 h sets the week"). Habit: long-ride days, reliable and shaky weekdays, life-load days. Goals use the shared active rule (weekly goals repeat).
- Weekly lift slots: `rules.weekly_slots` in the strength settings (set to A · D · B: Upper 1 · Lower · Upper 2) replaces the rotating pointer; empty keeps the rotation. While a leg area is protected the lower slot becomes the spare upper workout (C) with the reason. On save, and on the coach's `adjust_weekly_plan` edits, `fit_week_to_lift_slots` gives the week's lift days the slot workouts in order; done and past lifts use up their slot, bodyweight and travel lifts are left alone, and a coach-written gym session takes its slot workout. The athlete's own edits (week editor, Change today) and minimum weeks are not refitted. The coach reads the brief through `get_week_brief` (MCP), since `get_recent_context` can be too long to read in full.
- Planner: recent context has `week_brief` (this and next week); the plan and revision prompts tell the coach to follow it top to bottom and plan slot workouts by label.
- Plan (web): a Week brief section in the week card with the headline, the lift slots (a swapped slot dashed) and the first three rules, "Show all N rules" for the rest.
- Verified with `test_week_brief.py` (5) plus the arc suites; on live data next week reads "Base wk 1/5 · 3.5 h rides · Z2 only · 3 lifts · knee protected". Saved plans were not regenerated. Not on iOS yet.

### Winter arc editor and coach suggestion

2026-11-05: The Winter arc editor on Plan is a picture of the winter instead of a 10-column table, and the coach can draft the arc.
- Empty arc: "Suggest with coach" (primary) or "Set up myself" (template). In the editor, an optional note ("a trip, busy weeks, a goal…") goes with "Suggest with coach" / "Ask again".
- Chart: one bar per week, height = ride hours, coloured by block focus; lighter weeks are paler. Your last 8 weeks sit in grey to the left of the start, with a dashed "you ride ~2.5 h" line (6-week average). Block lanes under the bars carry name, hours and ride mix; months below.
- Clicking a bar or lane opens that block: inline name, focus segments, weeks and hours steppers, ride steppers per type (Z2, Sweet spot, VO2), lighter week Off / every 3 / 4 / 5, move earlier / later and remove. The coach's one-line reason shows on each block it suggested. The footer has the start Monday, Add block, Template, the total ("16 weeks · 57 h on the bike"), Cancel and Save arc.
- Suggestion: `GET /plans/winter-arc/suggestion-context` (`services/arc_suggest.py`) gives the last 12 full weeks (ride hours, rides, runs, lifts), FTP, active goals with commitment, illness in the last 60 days, life-load weeks ahead and the window (this or next Monday to the last Sunday of February). The coach helper's `/arc-suggestion` job (`scripts/arc_helper.py`, configured coach CLI) asks for back-to-back blocks that start near recent hours, keep anchor goals and fit the window, then clamps the reply to the editor's limits. Nothing is saved until Save arc.
- Verified with `test_arc_suggest.py` (3), `scripts/test_arc_helper.py` (6) and `tests/arc-draft.test.mjs` (5); a live suggestion at 1512 px returned Base → Sweet-spot build ×2 → VO2 touch → Maintain from 3 h, each with a reason. The arc was not saved.
