Body v3 · a reason to open it
Three different ideas for the Body screen (opened from the Readiness card on Today). Not a reskin of v2: each one answers a question you’d open the app for. Real data from 2 Nov unless marked. iPhone 17 Pro, 393 × 852 pt.
01Notesdiagnosis, principles, data
Why you don’t open Body
Last time we fixed colour and weight (Body v2, branch ios-body-v2) and it still didn’t pull. The problem isn’t only how it looks: the page gives you no reason to come back. Evidence: frame 02, the live screen with today’s data.
Diagnosis
  1. It repeats Today. Verdict, slice bar, “6 of 8 steady”: the Today card already said it. Opening Body adds a longer version of the same answer.
  2. It’s the same page every day. Eight text rows, six green ticks. Nothing says what changed since yesterday.
  3. It reads like a lab report. “7.6 h over 7 nights vs your 7.2 h median” has to be parsed before you feel anything.
  4. Nothing to do. No action, target or progress lives here. A page you can’t act on is a page you skip.
  5. It isn’t your body. No knee, no muscles, no comeback: the things you actually think about when you think “body”.
  6. Wins are invisible. HRV 56 (best this week) and your best night this week are green ticks in the same grey row as everything else.
  7. Bug on the way: the Sleep debt row prints last night (7.6 h) instead of the 7-night average (6.1 h), so a “risk” row reads as fine.
Principles for v3
  1. Lead with what changed since yesterday, not with the verdict you already saw.
  2. Show your body, not a table: a silhouette, your day, your knee.
  3. One thing to do on the page (volt): rehab now, lights out tonight.
  4. Celebrate wins with volt and big numbers, the way the weekly recap does.
  5. Details one tap away. The 8 signals stay, but below the fold or in a sheet.
  6. Flat: solid colour, tinted surfaces, no gradients or glow.
Mix and match
  • B is an entry point, A and C are pages. B (story each morning from the Today card) + A or C as the page underneath is a valid pick.
Data each direction needs
  • A · Body map: new muscles block on /phone/body (last trained + sets per muscle over 7 days, from logged sets with the exercise→muscle map the app already has). Knee from /recovery/issues (exists). Set counts here are illustrative.
  • B · Morning story: “highest this week” works from the 7 days we already get. “Highest in N weeks” needs a 30-day max on each vital (small backend add). Seen/unseen is local.
  • C · Body clock: sleep stages (exist), steps and heart by hour (HealthKit, already read), today’s session from /phone/today. Lights-out target is computed on the phone from your 7.2 h need and a wake time you set once. Hourly steps here are illustrative.
  • All numbers otherwise are today’s real ones: HRV 56, RHR 77 (+8), 7.6 h, debt 7 h 28 m.

04Direction Bmorning story
B · Morning story

Your night, told in 20 seconds. Full-screen story cards, one per thing that changed, wins first. Each card owns one colour, one giant number and one sentence of why.

Reason to open: it’s new every morning, and it’s short. The ring on the Today card tells you there’s something to watch.

  • Only signals that moved get a card (3–5 a day). Quiet days get one card: “Nothing changed, go train.”
  • Last card is today’s call + the plan + the one action.
  • Tap = next, hold = pause, swipe down = close.

05Direction Cbody clock
C · Body clock

Your day as a 24-hour dial. Last night’s sleep stages, steps per hour, training and the wind-down before lights out, with a hand for now.

Reason to open: it’s different every time you look. Morning shows the night, afternoon fills with steps, evening counts down to bed, and there’s a target you can hit tonight.

  • Sleep debt becomes a goal (lights out 23:30) instead of a red row.
  • Ticks on the evening card are the day’s small wins.
  • Tap the sleep arc for stages, a step bar for that hour.
