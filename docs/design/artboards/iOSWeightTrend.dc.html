Weight trend in the check-in
What's there today: weight is the last field in the check-in, with "Last: 80 kg on 2026-11-02" under it. A single weigh-in swings by a kilo (79 → 80 between Oct 30 and Nov 1), so one number can't tell you whether eating more is working, and it's the one thing the muscle-gain plan needs to know. The backend already works out the trend (/strength/muscle-gain: 7-day average, +0.25 kg a week, gentle gain), but only the web shows it. This adds a small chart under the field: your weigh-ins, the average line, and the lean-gain lane it should stay in, with today's number dropping in as you type.

Notes
Rules, data and build
Rules
  • Same maths as the web: the 7-day trailing average, the 6-week window, at least 4 weigh-ins, and the gentle-gain lane of 0.1–0.35 % of body weight a week. All from weight in GET /strength/muscle-gain; the phone redraws it, never recomputes it.
  • Today's ring: the phone adds the typed value to the last 7 days for the dashed end only; the status words don't change until it's saved.
  • Volt words are the only win here; Save stays the one volt button.
Data
  • Live on 3 Nov: weigh-ins Oct 12 79.0, Oct 18 78.3, Oct 25 80.5, Oct 29 80.0, Oct 30 79.0, Nov 1 80.0, Nov 2 80.0; average 79.75; +0.25 kg a week; lane 0.08–0.28; status gentle gain.
  • Made up: today's 79.6, and the flat, fast and sparse states.
  • Backend: nothing new; /strength/muscle-gain already returns weight. If it turns out slow on the phone, a ?only=weight variant is a new route, not a change.
Build
  • A WeightTrendView in the check-in sheet, fed by a small DTO; decoding and the "typed point" maths are unit-tested.
  • -checkinDemo trend|flat|fast|sparse draws each state from canned data, with no writes.
  • The "Last: … on 2026-11-02" line goes away (the ring and the dots replace it).
