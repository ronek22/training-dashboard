00 · Notes · rules, widgets and data

Rules

  • Same voice as the phone widgets v2. The sport leads as a coloured kicker, one italic title, one plain line. Time in yellow ●, protein teal ●, kcal bar volt.
  • Volt only for wins. Done, target hit. Nothing else on the wrist is volt.
  • Tap = the next step. Today and Rehab open straight onto the Watch app's Start card for that session (Start frame 01). Week and Food open the Start screen; the Watch app has no food or week screen and won't get one.
  • Shapes carry meaning, so tinted faces still work. Ring = amount (week hours, kcal), segments = checklist (rehab), seven dots = week. In a tinted face every colour turns into the face tint and these shapes still read.
  • Flat card. #12151C container, no border, no gradient. OLED black around it.
  • During a workout no widget is needed. The Watch app stays frontmost; the rest timer is already there. Not in this round: a Control Center / Action button control.

Smart Stack order (relevance)

  • Morning to session done: Today on top.
  • Rehab planned and not ticked by 18:00: Rehab rises above Today.
  • After 18:00 and under 60 % eaten: Food rises (the under-eating focus).
  • Session done: Today flips to the Done card with tomorrow, then sinks after 2 h.
  • Rest day / sick day: Today shows tomorrow; Week sits second.
