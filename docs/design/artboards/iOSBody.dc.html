Body v2 · why it looks washed out
Evidence: simulator screenshots of the live app with today’s real data (frame 02, left columns).
Diagnosis
  1. Colour is diluted, not owned. Vital tiles sit on 8% tints and the range bands on 14–16%. On #12151C that reads as muddy navy, brown and plum (the RHR band and the step bars at 40% look brown), not as blue, pink, violet.
  2. Metric colours collide with warning colours. Resting HR is drawn in the risk red #FF8A8A and steps in the caution amber #FFC76E. On a normal day every card already looks like an alert, so a real alert doesn’t stand out.
  3. The tone colours are pastel. #FF8A8A and #FFC76E are light and low in saturation. “RECOVER” in pastel coral on grey looks soft, not urgent.
  4. No hero. Readiness is a word plus a 4 pt slice bar. Nothing has the weight of a score ring or a 50 pt number. Every card has the same weight, so the eye has nowhere to land.
  5. Grey wins. Most text is muted grey labels, detail lines and axis letters. The colour that exists sits in 7–9 pt dots and 1.5 pt strokes.
  6. Charts are too thin. 4 pt bars, 9 pt dots, faint bands. The 7-day charts have no line joining the days, so the trend is hard to read.
  7. Above the fold on Today only the “RECOVER” word and the slice bar show; the vitals sit under the tab bar.
Principles
  1. One owning colour per metric, used at full strength: ring, bars, line, icon chip and kicker. Same mapping on Today and Body.
  2. Tones are saturated and only mean status: steady #2ED3B0, caution #FFC83A, risk #FF5A6E (the values the Activity detail badges already use). No metric is drawn in a tone colour.
  3. A hero on every card: one big Barlow number (36–52 pt) with a small Archivo unit, or the signal ring.
  4. Solid marks, tinted surfaces. Strokes 10–14 pt on rings, 2.5–3 pt lines, bars 7–26 pt wide. Surfaces 14–18% tint, never less. No gradients, glows or blur.
  5. Volt only for wins and the one action: the “Good to go” pill and Start session. Check-in stays an outline button.
  6. Fewer grey words. Status says itself in colour (“Low · −75%” in red); “Typical” stays quiet.
  7. Section headers outside the cards (coloured kicker + italic title) give Body a rhythm, like the Aether reference.
  8. The top 190 pt of the Today card must carry the verdict, because that is what shows above the tab bar under the session card.

Data: what exists, what doesn’t
  • No 0–100 readiness score. The backend returns a level and penalty points (red ≥ 4, amber ≥ 2). The ring therefore shows signals: one segment per factor, coloured by tone, “5/8 steady” in the centre. No backend change needed. If you want a number instead, the backend could add score = max(0, 100 − 12 × points) to /phone/today and /phone/body; I’d rather not, because it suggests a precision we don’t have.
  • Sleep stages exist (watch segments), so the segmented bar and timeline use real data.
  • No stress data, so no gauge.
  • Today’s steps (6,240) and the hourly steps and heart-rate shapes are examples: at screenshot time (02:07) it was 208 steps and the simulator has no live Health data. Every other number is from /phone/body today.
Surfaces
Kept: bg #0A0C11, card #12151C with #1C212B border, inset #0E1117. Cards stay bordered; the tint goes on chips, tiles and bands instead.
