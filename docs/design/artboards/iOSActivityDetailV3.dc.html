01 · Notes · diagnosis and the D2 principles

Why v2 looked unfinished

  1. No hero, just an instrument panel. The first card stacked 2 numbers, 2 zone gauges with 8 tiny axis labels and a footer row. Nothing led.
  2. Ten equal grey boxes. Same card, grey caps kicker, no titles; Load and Session read put boxes inside the box.
  3. Things said twice. NP/TSS in the gauge and in Load; for strength, the win and the session read shared a headline.
  4. Weak charts, colour without meaning. A solid navy area with no axes; zones starting grey; a volt border on a secondary card.
  5. Uneven rhythm, buried action. 12/16/18 pt spacing, 9–11 pt labels, and "Log how it felt" at the very bottom.

D2 principles

  1. A visual leads every page. The map for outdoor activities (dark, glowing route coloured by pace, speed or elevation), the power or HR trace for indoor and physio, the muscle map for strength. It fades into the page, with the title and one big number on it.
  2. One title, then calm sections. Only the activity name is big. The session read is a normal section, its verdict a sentence.
  3. Colour per metric, everywhere. Time yellow, distance cyan, pace/speed orange, HR red, power violet, elevation green, cadence blue, volume pink: the same in the grid, the charts and the tables.
  4. Numbers explain themselves. Every grid value has a status line (teal tick, amber !, grey ring).
  5. Sections on the background, not boxes. Hairlines and 32 pt rhythm; units at 58% size.
  6. Depth behind "Show more". Each section is a summary; a sheet holds the full chart, scrub, zones and extras.
  7. One primary action. Volt only for the win icon and "Log how it felt" (when there's no feedback).
  8. Motion with meaning. The route and the trace draw in once on open; static under Reduce Motion.
Backend: one additive field, run_dynamics on GET /activities/{id}, with this run's form averages and per-km splits. Both are already computed from HealthFit FIT files into the run_dynamics table; your usual values and the good ranges already come from /running/fitness. Runs without a FIT file skip Splits and Running form. Phone-only additions (no backend): decoding workout_execution for plan-match lines, muscles from exercise names (Muscles.swift), pace and speed colouring from the existing chart streams. Left for later: hike marked-trail colours (Mountains data).
