Notes
Phase 2 · rule fixes
Critique
  • Today: the solid chip competes with Start session; correction to the audit: volt there is a win, which the rules allow. The fix is weight, not colour.
  • Food: volt for a half-full ring says "done" when the focus is under-eating. Volt should only appear when the ring closes.
  • Plan: two coach entry points side by side; the header has more buttons than any other tab.
  • Coach: rows a third taller than every other list; section kicker misaligned.
Build
  • WinsRow: pill fill volt.opacity(0.12), text volt, check disc solid volt with dark tick.
  • KcalRing: eaten arc and planned-dinner arc in ink while toGo > 0; volt + "On target" when closed. Legend swatches follow.
  • PlanView header: one HeaderIconButton with a Menu; refine item hidden when no editable days; spinner state opens the sheet directly.
  • CoachView: listRowInsets top/bottom 4 on rows; kicker .padding(.leading, -12) to land at 20 pt.
Data
  • Live on 3 Nov: streak 304, protein 33/130 g, 1,173 of 2,478 kcal, macros, week 45 plan, coach conversation titles.
  • Made up: the closed ring (2,512 kcal), one coach row (Indoor ride).
  • Session and readiness cards abbreviated; tab bar matches the app on 3 Nov.
