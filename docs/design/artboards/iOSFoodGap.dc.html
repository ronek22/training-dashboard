Close the gap, eat it again
What's there today: Food logs well, but every meal costs a scan or a description, even the soup you've had four times this month, and in the evening the ring says how much is missing but not what would fix it. Ride fuelling lives only on the web plan. This adds three things, all on Food: an Again row of meals you repeat (one tap), a Close the gap card in the evening with two foods from your own log drawn onto the gap they'd close, and a fuel note on ride days that need one.

Notes
Rules and data
Again row
  • Slot by clock: breakfast < 11, lunch < 16, dinner < 22, else snack. First card: yesterday's entry for that slot (if any).
  • Favourites: entries from the last 30 days grouped by their item names (folded, so jagnięciny/jagnięciną match), seen ≥ 2×, most frequent first, plus saved foods. Up to 5 cards; the row scrolls.
  • Logging copies the items (grams, macros) into a new entry for today, source: "repeat". No Claude call.
  • Already eaten today → card hidden, so a tap can't double-log by accident.
Close the gap
  • Gap = target − eaten − planned dinner (same as the web ideas service). Shows after 17:00 if protein ≥ 15 g or kcal ≥ 300 short.
  • Candidates: past entries and single items you've logged, plus saved foods and products, at portions ×½, 1, 1½, 2. Scored with the existing meal_ideas scoring (no overshoot past 115 %, protein weight while protein is open, carbs on/after a key ride). Repeats of today's food sink.
  • Two picks, different main foods.
Data and backend
  • New GET /phone/food/quick?date&hour → again[], gap (left, picks with items), fuel (today's fuel plan from fuelling.build_fuel_plan). New route only; nothing the web uses changes.
  • Live: targets (2 478 / 130 g), 2 Nov breakfast, October repeats (pomidorówka 4×, kurczak słodko-kwaśny 4×), item values, Sunday's sweet spot and its fuel plan.
  • Made up: today's eaten numbers, times, Sunday's breakfast and evening.
  • Eaten card, timeline and Scan buttons as in the app on main (3 Nov).
