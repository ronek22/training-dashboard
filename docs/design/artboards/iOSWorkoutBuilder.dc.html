Leaving with changes
Cancel with unsaved edits asks once: Save · Discard · Keep editing. Drafts survive the app closing mid-gym, and saves queue offline until the Mac is back.
Reordering gets its own calm mode (the ⇅ button in frame 14, or long-press any row): rows shrink to one line so the whole workout fits, the lifted row tilts with a haptic tick on each slot, and the slot it will land in is outlined. In normal mode rows stay tappable without fighting a drag.
Why this shape. You build workouts standing at a rack between sets, one-handed. So: sheets over full-screen pushes, steppers and chips over keyboards, your history over a generic library, and the same controls as the session screen.
Taken from Fitbod, made ours. Body map of muscles hit · swap for the same muscle · long-press to reorder. Not taken: AI-generated workouts, recovery percentages, video library, their blue-on-white cards.
Backend fit. Saving uses the existing POST/PUT /templates. Picks come from /exercise-suggestions. New: sets per muscle in the template response via muscle_groups_for, muscle tags on suggestions, and a template-from-session helper. Silhouette paths are the web's MuscleSilhouette.vue.
Left out on purpose. Supersets, per-set weight ramps and warm-up schemes. The template model has none of them, and the web stays the place for heavy planning. Phase 2 if you miss them.
