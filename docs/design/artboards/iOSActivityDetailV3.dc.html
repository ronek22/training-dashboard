01 · Notes · why the web looks finished and the phone doesn't

Diagnosis

  1. No hero, just a dense instrument panel. The first card stacks 2 big numbers, 2 zone gauges (marker, tick, range, 4 axis labels each in 10 pt) and a footer row. That's five type sizes in one card, and the eye has nowhere to land. The web opens with one band of 4–5 large numbers, each with a single sub-line. v2 screen 1 · web "Ride performance"
  2. Ten boxes, all the same weight. Every module is the same 22 pt-radius grey card with a grey uppercase kicker and no title. Load and Session read then nest boxes inside the box (6 bordered tiles, 4 bordered signals). The web gives every section a coloured kicker plus a Barlow title ("Session analysis", "Time in zones") and puts secondary stats in one quiet inline row. v2 screens 2–3 · Load card
  3. Things are said twice. NP and TSS show in the headline gauge and again in Load. Avg power shows in the gauge and in the chart header. For strength, the win strip and the Session read have the same headline ("3 of 5 lifts up on last time"). v2 Zwift · v2 Workout A
  4. The charts are weaker than the web's. v2 draws a heavy solid navy area with an HR line on top and no gridlines, y-labels or average line. The web uses a 1.75 px line, a 14% flat fill, faint gridlines with labels, a dashed average with a value pill, and elevation as a ghost behind. v2 "Power & heart rate" vs web "Session analysis"
  5. Colour doesn't follow the web. v2 zones run grey → teal → yellow (Z1 grey), but the web uses blue → green → amber → orange → red. Gauge tracks show the zone rainbow at 28% opacity. The not-requested Coach card gets a volt border, though volt is only for wins and the one primary action. v2 zones · coach card
  6. Uneven rhythm. 12 pt gaps everywhere, 16/18 pt padding mixed, and labels at 9, 10 and 11 pt. Section edges blur together when you scroll. The web uses 20+ px between sections, a consistent 24 px card padding and a clear title → sentence → data order. v2 Load tiles 9 pt labels · gauge axis 10 pt
  7. The one action is buried. "Log how it felt" is the most useful tap after a session, but it sits at the bottom of v2. The web puts it first and makes it volt. web Afternoon Ride / Physio

v3 principles

  1. One hero per sport. The top card shows 2 numbers at 56 pt (the session's identity), then 3 tiles at 28 pt and one inline row. Routes sit inside the hero card for run, hike and outdoor ride, so map and numbers read as one unit.
  2. Sections, not boxes. Every card is kicker (sport colour) → Barlow title 22 → one plain sentence → data. Inside a card: hairlines, never a box in a box (except the hero tiles and feel meters, which are inputs or values to compare).
  3. Say it once. Load merges into the hero (NP · IF · TSS tiles, VI · kJ · drift inline). When the win and the session read share a headline, the win shows as a line inside Session read.
  4. Charts the web way. 1.75 pt line in the series colour, flat 14% fill, gridlines #1F242E with 10 pt labels, dashed average with a pill, elevation ghost at 7%, scrub pill above the plot. No gradients.
  5. Colour means one thing. Sport colour = identity (pill, hero accent, kickers, primary series). Metric dots follow the web (power violet, intensity orange, HR red). Zones use the web palette. Volt = win + the single primary action. Red = pain/warn only.
  6. One spacing scale: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32. 16 between cards and inside cards, 12 inside groups, 8 between chips/tiles. Type ramp: 56 / 34 / 28 / 22 / 17 / 15 / 14 / 13 / 12 / 11 (kicker) / 10 (axis).
  7. One primary action. No feedback yet → "Log how it felt" (volt) moves up under the hero. Once logged, How it felt drops back to its v2 slot and nothing on the page is volt except the win.
  8. iOS-native chrome. Floating 44 pt circular back/share buttons over the content, collapsing to an inline title on scroll. Segmented controls for chart and zone tabs. Map tap opens a full-screen sheet.
