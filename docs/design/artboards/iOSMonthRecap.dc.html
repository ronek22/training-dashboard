Month in review
What's there today: the Sunday recap tells you whether a week happened, and the web has a monthly letter (wins + one focus, in prose). Neither answers is my training working? Weight, strength, aerobic base and run fitness move over months, and today each lives on a different web page. The week recap's 8-week bar chart is time only: it can't tell a strong month from a long one. Proposal: a month page in the week recap's language (big count-ups, cards that lift in), with the letter's wins and focus, and four signal cards that each carry one picture. October's numbers are live.
