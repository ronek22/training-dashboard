06f · Interactions & states
Scrub. Press and drag on any chart: a vertical rule and a pill with time · main value · HR. Light haptic tick each minute.
Map. Tap → full-screen sheet (MapKit, standard dark). Tap a best effort → its segment glows on the map.
Zones. HR ↔ Power toggle only when both exist. Rows under 2% merge into one “Z4+” row.
Coach. Saved analysis: headline, 3-line summary, “N observations ›” expands. Pending: spinner row “Coach is writing…”. Not requested: outline Ask the coach (POST /activities/{id}/analysis).
Loading. Header + hero stats render at once from the list row; the rest shows skeleton cards. Offline: cached detail with “last synced” under the title.
Not on the phone. Laps, segments, muscle map, plan-match editing and the coach chat stay on the web page.
