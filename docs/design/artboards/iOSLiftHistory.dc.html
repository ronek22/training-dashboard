Lift history, warm-ups and plates
What's there today: the session screen shows one set at a time, with the coach's hint under the lift name ("Travel today: holding 77.5 kg… Match last time") but not what last time actually was. To see it you'd open the web app, and the brief before the session is gone once you start. Barbell loads are left to mental maths (77.5 kg on your 10 kg bar = 20 + 5 + 5 + 2.5 + 1.25 a side), and there's no warm-up: the first set you log is already the working weight. This adds a one-line history under the lift name that opens a small chart of your last sessions, a plate picture inside the set card for barbell lifts, and a warm-up ramp before the first heavy lift that you tick off without logging.

Warm-up · when it shows
First barbell lift of the session, working weight ≥ 40 kg. Not on dumbbell days, bodyweight days or rehab.
Steps: bar × 10, ~50 % × 5, ~70 % × 3, ~85 % × 2, rounded to 2.5 kg, dropping any step that repeats or falls under the 10 kg bar. Resuming a session where set 1 is logged skips it.
The line and plates adapt to the lift. Each shows what that kind of lift is measured by. Lifts with no history, and loads the plates can't make, say so plainly.
Notes
Rules, data and build
Rules
  • History: last 4 completed sessions of the same lift (name match), working sets only. Top load per session, or best reps/hold when there's no load.
  • Barbell lifts: named Barbell…, Back/Front Squat, Deadlift or Romanian Deadlift (not Dumbbell…), or Bench/Overhead Press without "Dumbbell". Others get no plates and no warm-up.
  • Plates: your equipment from the Athlete page (GET /settings/athlete-profile): 10 kg bar, plates 20 × 2, 5 × 4, 2.5 × 4, 1.25 × 8. Heaviest first, never more than you own; the most you can load is 90 kg, and above that it says so.
  • Warm-up ticks live on the phone for this session only. Never logged or uploaded.
Data
  • Live on 3 Nov: Back Squat on Sep 2 (80 × 6 × 3), Sep 10 (60 × 6 × 2), Sep 22 (75 × 6 × 3), Oct 15 (77.5 × 6 × 3); best 80 kg; template D target 77.5 × 6 and its hint; split squat 16 × 8.
  • Made up: how each set felt, the clocks, and the pull-up and plank history.
  • Backend: new GET /strength/workouts/exercise-history?name=&limit=4 → sessions with date, sets (weight, reps, seconds, effort). New route plus a test; nothing the web uses changes.
Build
  • History for every lift in the session is fetched once at start and cached, so it works offline once loaded.
  • Plate maths and the warm-up ramp are pure functions with unit tests (StrengthLogic).
  • -strengthDemo set|warmup|history draws these frames from canned data, with no session on the Mac.
  • The Watch is untouched in this pass.
