Morning brief and wins
What's there today: the 7:30 nudge says "How did you sleep?" with nothing about the day, at a fixed time whether you're up or not. On Today, done things just disappear: the check-in button goes away, rehab ticks inside the session card, protein isn't on Today at all. Only the flame says "well done", and it counts days, not today. This adds one push when you wake that says what today is and why, and makes finishing things look like a win: check-in and protein in one quiet row under the title, the workout and rehab inside the session card where they already live.

Notes
Brief text, rules and data
Morning brief
  • Trigger: the HealthKit sleep observer that already wakes the app. Once a day between 04:00 and 11:00, after the Health push succeeds, so readiness includes last night.
  • Title: the session and its length, Knee rehab · 27 min on rehab-only days, Rest day, or Sick mode.
  • Body: sleep hours, then the why: green → the plan's purpose; amber → go easy; red → the off signals and the short version. Ends with "Tap to check in" until it's in.
  • Off the LAN it uses the cached week for the title and skips readiness.
  • Settings › Reminders: the check-in row becomes Morning brief, "after you wake, or at 07:30".
Which wins, where
  • Row: check-in (every day) and protein (once a target exists). Sick mode: same two.
  • Session card: the workout (part 1) and rehab (part 2, only while a recovery issue has a routine). Their badges flip to volt and burst when done. No new slots, no duplicates.
  • Rest day: the row only; the rest-day card stays as it is.
  • Clean day: the row and every part of the session card done.
  • Celebrated once per win per day (kept on the phone). Reduce Motion: just the fill.
Data
  • Live on 3 Nov: session, readiness (Recover, HRV 40, RHR 77, sleep 7.6 h), rehab 8 exercises, protein target 130 g, 2 Nov protein 97 g.
  • Made up: mid-day progress (rehab 3/8), the evening states, the clean-day count, 7:04.
  • Backend: add protein (eaten_g, target_g) to GET /phone/today so Today needs no extra request. New field only; nothing the web uses changes.
  • Session card abbreviated; readiness card and tab bar match the app as of 3 Nov.
  • Row (03): done wins fold into one volt pill ("Check-in", "2 done", "All done"); open ones are a 22 pt ring and a soft number. Tap anything in the row to open it.
