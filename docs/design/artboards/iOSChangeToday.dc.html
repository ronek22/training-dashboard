Change today with the coach
Critique of the card today: three outline pills compete under Start session, and none of them can change what the day is. Short on time only swaps to a pre-written version; Move day pushes the whole thing away; Other workout leaves the plan. The plan never hears that you felt rough, had an extra half hour or wanted to ride. Readiness is red this morning and the card can't respond. This adds one way to ask the coach to rewrite today, with four quick asks so it's two taps, not typing. The signature visual is a now / ask tape: today's session as a bar, and a ghost of what you're asking for.

Notes
Flow, backend and data
Flow
  • Change today (03 → 04/05/06 → 07 → 08 → 09). One job at a time, shared with the Plan tab's coach.
  • Picking an ask + chip writes a sentence (“Ride instead, ~45 min.”); the free note is appended. That's the feedback sent.
  • Short on time with a plan fallback: instant, no coach (today's behaviour, now inside the sheet). Without one, it asks the coach.
  • Move it to another day opens the existing Move day sheet.
  • Rest pick asks the coach to make today rest; it may move the session later in the week.
Backend
  • No new route needed: POST /plans/weekly/coach/revise with target_date = today already exists and may only touch that day (Plan day's “Change with coach” uses it).
  • Open question: the coach may want to move the dropped lift to another day (08's paragraph). With target_date it can't. Either keep it strictly one-day, or send a refine of the rest of the week with “start from today”. I'd keep one-day for v1.
  • Readiness and its drivers come from /widget/today, already loaded.
Data
